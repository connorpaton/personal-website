These are things I want to live by. Writing them down is easier than following them, which is part of why I want them here.

## Irrational Self-Belief

If I don't believe in what I'm doing, why should anyone else?

I want enough belief to begin before I have everyone's approval. That doesn't mean ignoring evidence. It means not needing permission to take myself seriously.

## Overflow

Be steady first. Then give.

I want to be useful to the people around me without treating being depleted as proof that I care.

## Let Them

I can't control what other people choose. I can pay attention to those choices and decide how to respond.

Less trying to manage everyone. More observing what's actually there.

## Law of Inertia

An object in motion tends to stay in motion.

I think about this when starting feels harder than the thing itself. Do something small enough to begin. Give yourself something to build on.

## Be Kind

Kindness matters even when it doesn't get noticed or lead anywhere useful.

I don't want to need a strategic reason to treat someone well.

## Be Curious

Question longer than others.

Especially when I think I already understand. There's usually more to learn if I'm willing to stop performing certainty for a minute.

## Strangers Are Just Friends You Haven't Met Yet

Stay open. Most people carry something worth knowing.

I don't know who someone will become in my life before we've had a conversation. I'd rather leave room to find out.

## Be Contrarian

Consensus deserves scrutiny, not automatic rejection.

I want to know why I agree or disagree. Being different for the sake of it doesn't tell me much.

## Choose the Harder Choice

It's usually the one I'm tempted to talk myself out of for the wrong reasons.

Not everything difficult is worthwhile. But when I know what the right choice is, I don't want convenience to make the decision for me.
