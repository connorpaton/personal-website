---
title: "On Waiting to Be Ready"
description: "Preparation matters. Waiting to feel certain is something else."
date: "2024-12-23"
---

> "It's a terrible thing, I think, in life to wait until you're ready."
> - Hugh Laurie

This has stuck with me since I first heard it.

It's easy to make waiting sound responsible. A little more preparation. A better moment. Some future version of yourself who knows exactly what to do.

Preparation matters. But I think there's a point where it stops being preparation and starts being a way to avoid the discomfort of beginning.

The question I take from this quote is simple: what am I actually waiting for?

If there's something specific I need to learn or sort out, fine. If I'm waiting for uncertainty to disappear, I might be waiting a long time.

I don't read this as permission to be careless. Just a reminder that feeling ready isn't always what comes first.
